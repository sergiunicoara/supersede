# Packaging and labelling procedure (revision 2)

Finished castings are packed {{packaging.units_per_box = 24}} to a box. Every box carries a label
in {{packaging.label_language = English and German}}. Boxes are sealed with
{{packaging.seal = tamper-evident tape}}.
