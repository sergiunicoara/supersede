[First-page note (added to the PDF after the 2026 rule)]
The requirements related to complaint handling found in the previous QS regulation are now described in ISO 13485:2016, Clause 8.2.2. Additional FDA requirements that apply to complaint records are set forth at 21 CFR 820.35(a).

[Body]
Please note: The Quality System (QS) regulation requires you to have a formally designated unit to receive, review and evaluate complaints [21 CFR 820.198(a)].

[Body]
The QS regulation requires that complaints be investigated by a formally designated unit [21 CFR 820.198]. Manufacturers must evaluate events that may be subject to MDR requirements in accordance with the complaint file requirements in 21 CFR 820.198 [21 CFR 803.18(e)].

[Body]
We will not consider an MDR report that you submit to be in compliance with the MDR regulation unless you evaluate the event or complaint in accordance with 21 CFR 820.198 of the QS regulation regarding investigation of device complaints [21 CFR 803.18(e)].

[Body]
MDR files should be maintained for two years from the date of the event or a period equivalent to the expected life of the device, whichever is greater [see 21 CFR 803.18(c)], even if the event was determined to be not reportable or you have stopped manufacturing or distributing the device.
